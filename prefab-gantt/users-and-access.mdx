Admins manage access from the user menu, under Manage users & roles.

## Getting someone in

Anyone with an eganco.com Google account can sign in, but signing in does not give them anything. Until they have a role, they land on a screen that says Access not yet granted, with Check again and Sign out buttons.

There are two ways to give someone access:

- If they show up in the People list, press Grant on their row, pick a role and a scope, and save.
- If they do not, use Grant access by email at the bottom. Enter their eganco.com address, a role and a scope, and press Grant. If they have signed in before, it takes effect right away. If not, it waits for their first sign-in.

The scope is a line for workers and leads, a location for managers, and the entire org for admins. See [Roles and permissions](/prefab-gantt/roles-and-permissions).

Once access is granted, the person can press Check again, or sign out and back in.

## The People list

The list shows Person, Email, Role, Status and Last sign-in. Problems sort to the top. The statuses are:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Has access | Signed in and has a role |
| Signed in — no access | Signed in at least once, but nobody has given them a role |
| Invited — not signed in yet | Granted by email, waiting for their first sign-in |
| Invite will never fire | An email invite is still listed for someone who already has access. Revoke the invite |
| No sign-in account | Has a role in the app, but no sign-in account to go with it |
| Account disabled | Their sign-in account is disabled |
| Access removed | Access was taken away |

Import employee directory refreshes the list of people from the HR directory. It adds active employees and drops inactive ones. It does not give anyone access.

## Changing or removing access

Edit on a row changes the role and scope. Remove takes access away and asks for a reason, for example that the person left the company. Pending email invites can be revoked from the same list.

A few changes are refused:

- Nobody can change their own access. Another admin has to.
- Nobody can give a role above their own, or a scope outside their own.
- The last working admin cannot be removed or demoted.

A role change can take up to an hour to reach someone who is not in the app. Having them sign out and back in should pick it up right away.

## Other admin tools

Digest preview, in the user menu, shows what the daily mention email would say, without sending anything.

Admins can remove a comment that was a test, wrong or rude. The app asks for a reason, and the thread shows Comment removed by an admin in its place.
