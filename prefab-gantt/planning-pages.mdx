title: "Planning pages"
description: "The Planning menu: resources, time off, apprentices, holidays, forecasted jobs and status colors."
---

The Planning menu holds the shop's capacity data. Together these pages decide how many hours the shop has on a given day, which is what the schedule packs tickets into.

## Who can edit which page

| Page | Who can edit |
| --- | --- |
| Resource List | Manager and up |
| Holidays | Manager and up |
| Status Colors | Manager and up |
| Time Off | Lead and up |
| Apprentice Calendar | Lead and up |
| Forecasted Jobs | Lead and up |

## Saving

These pages do not save as you type. Edits collect in a bar at the bottom that reads N unsaved changes, with Discard and Save. Leaving the page with unsaved edits asks first.

If someone else saved the same page while you were editing, the app tells you. Saving keeps your changes and leaves their other rows alone. Discard takes their version instead.

## Resource List

One row per person: Name, % Billable, Crew, Classification and Can Shop Lead. Can Shop Lead decides who shows up in the Shop Lead picker on a ticket.

## Time Off

Employee, Time Off Start, Time Off End and Location / PTO. Time off comes out of the shop's available hours.

## Apprentice Calendar

Employee, Start Date, End Date and School Day. The app works out the school days from those, and they come out of available hours like time off.

## Holidays

Holiday Name, Date, Hours Off and Working Day?. Hours Off can be part of a day, from 0 to 8. A holiday can apply to one line, a location or the whole org, and the editor needs manager rights at that level.

The page also checks the list against the usual holiday rules and offers to add ones that are missing or fix dates that look wrong. Nothing changes until you accept a suggestion.

Holidays also matter outside the schedule. The VDC Due Date skips them, so a missing holiday counts as a working day and pushes due dates around it a day later than they should be.

## Forecasted Jobs

Work that is expected but not in SIS yet: Job Description, PM, Date Requested, Est. Hours, Hrs/Day, Days, Show in Gantt?, Ticket Date, Custom Start, Sat Hrs, Sun Hrs, Foreman and Job Cost. Rows with Show in Gantt? ticked feed the soft backlog on Rolling Charts.

## Status Colors

One row per SIS status: Status, Color, Preview (as rendered), Source and Work finished. Clearing a color puts the status back on the built-in color. A status that no ticket uses right now is marked unused but kept, so its color is still there if it comes back.

The colors are shop-wide, so changing one changes it for everyone.
