## The Analytics page

Analytics has these sections:

- Status Tracker: one card per status. Clicking a card filters the whole app to that status.
- Rolling hours, Resource Utilization, Over-Budget and hours by PM.
- VDC On Time at Handoff. See [VDC on time at handoff](/prefab-gantt/vdc-on-time).

## The report builder

With the Report browser switch on (it is off by default, see [Settings and switches](/prefab-gantt/settings-and-switches)), the report builder replaces most of the page. The Status Tracker and the VDC section stay where they are.

The builder has three datasets: Tickets, Scheduled hours and VDC Handoffs. It starts with three reports, Backlog, Schedule & Capacity and Over Budget, which can be copied and changed.

Saved reports are kept in your browser only. Nobody else sees them, and they do not follow you to another computer.

## What history it covers

The builder works on the live schedule: tickets that are open now and the hours planned ahead. Completed tickets are not in it.

The VDC Handoffs dataset is the exception, because it is a record of past handoffs. It covers the same handoffs as the VDC section, from October 27, 2026 on.
