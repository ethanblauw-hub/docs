## Comments

Anyone with access can comment on a ticket, in the Activity section of its detail panel. Type @ to mention someone, and Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) posts it.

Only the person who wrote a comment can edit it, and an edited comment says (edited). Only an admin can remove one.

## Mentions

Mentioning someone puts the comment in their Mentions tray, under the bell in the header. Opening a mention marks it read, and the tray has Mark all read and Dismiss.

A ticket's PM and field leaders can be mentioned even if they do not use the app. They get the mention by email instead.

Editing a comment to add a mention notifies only the people who were added.

## Push notifications

Push notifications are set per browser, under Push notifications in the user menu. They are off until you turn them on, and the browser asks for permission when you do. The toggle shows On, Off or Blocked.

Blocked means the browser is refusing. If you declined the prompt, allow notifications again from the padlock in the address bar. If it says the block came from your administrator, that is a company browser policy, and only IT can change it.

## Email

Email me, in the user menu, has two settings that work on their own:

| Setting | Default | What it sends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When I am mentioned | Off | One email per mention, with a short snippet of the comment and a link to the ticket |
| A daily summary of what I have not read | On | One email on weekday mornings at 5:00 am Central |

The per-mention email's subject is "Mentioned on" and the ticket. Replying to it goes to the person who wrote the comment.

The daily summary lists unread mentions: new ones since the last summary, and older unread ones still waiting. It is only sent when there is something new. Comment snippets and links are only included for tickets the person can see.

Email only goes to company addresses.
