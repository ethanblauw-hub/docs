## Saved views

A saved view remembers the page you are on with its filters, sort, hidden columns and, on the queues, the search and the Mine toggle. Saved Views is in the header.

Everyone can keep up to 10 views of their own, with names up to 60 characters. Saving under a name you already used offers to save a copy. At 10, you pick one to replace.

Star a view to make it your default. Reset goes back to it. Clear filters clears the sort, filters and hidden columns on the page you are on.

## Sharing and publishing

Leads and above can share a view with specific people, up to 25. Managers and above can publish a view to the whole shop.

Other people cannot dismiss a published view. Only a manager can unpublish it.

If a saved view points at something that has since been removed or renamed, like a column or a status, the app applies the rest and says Part of that view no longer matches. Saving the view again fixes it.

## Export

The Export menu in the header has four choices:

| Choice | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| CSV spreadsheet | The rows and columns on screen, as shown |
| Calendar feed (.ics) | One calendar event per ticket |
| Print / PDF | The visible part of the Gantt |
| PNG image | A picture of the current view |

The CSV follows what is on screen, so filter and hide columns first if you only want part of it.
