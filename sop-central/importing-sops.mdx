---

On a new SOP, Import from File opens the Import SOP dialog. It takes a PDF, a plain text file, or a video, and the AI drafts the title, purpose, scope and steps from it.

It is a starting point, not a finished SOP. Read every step before submitting it, because the AI does get things wrong, especially numbers and part names.

## Images

For a PDF, the pictures in the document are pulled out and attached to the step they belong to. Images it cannot match to a step are left in a sidebar so you can drag them where they go. A 9-page PDF with a screenshot per page takes around 10 seconds.

If an import comes back with no images at all from a PDF that clearly has them, let me know. That has been a real bug before, and it is not something you did.

## Limits

Documents are capped at 14 MB. A bigger file gets a message asking you to split it into smaller sections. Videos over 200 MB get a warning that they may fail, and they often do.

The import is not saved until you save it. Images from an import you abandon are cleaned up after 48 hours.
