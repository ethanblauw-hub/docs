A few features are behind switches that only admins can change. Everyone can see their effect, but only admins can flip them.

| Switch | Where | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email Delivery | Settings, Maintenance | Paused, Testing or Live email. See [Notifications and email](/sop-central/notifications-and-email). | Paused |
| Let authors assign reviewers | Settings, Approval Groups | Turns on named reviewers. See [Reviewing and approving](/sop-central/reviewing-and-approving#named-reviewers). | Off |
| Scan SOPs on save | Settings, Definitions | Attaches glossary terms to a SOP when it is saved. | Off |
| AI flowcharts | No switch in the app | Shows Generate flowchart on published SOPs. | Off, but it is on right now |

The AI flowchart switch does not have a place in Settings yet. Ask me if it needs to change.

## Templates

Settings, Templates holds the print template and logo used when a SOP is printed or exported. These are saved in your browser, not in the app. Two admins can end up with different templates, and clearing your browser data resets yours.

## Maintenance

Besides Email Delivery, the Maintenance tab has a few tools that are mostly for me:

- Point-in-Time Recovery restores a single SOP to how it was at a chosen time, up to 7 days back. It is the thing to reach for if a SOP was saved over by mistake.
- Re-index Search Database rebuilds what the SOP Assistant searches. Use it if the Assistant cannot find a SOP that is published.
- Data Migration runs one-off data changes. Do not run these unless I ask.
