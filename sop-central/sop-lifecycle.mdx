A SOP is always in one of four states: Draft, In Review, Published or Archived.

| From | To | How | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft | In Review | Submit for Review | Anyone who can edit it |
| In Review | Draft | Save it back as a draft | Anyone who can edit it |
| In Review | Published | Approve & Publish | Approvers, or the named reviewers |
| Draft, In Review or Published | Archived | Archive | Approvers |
| Archived | Draft | Restore to Drafts | Approvers |
| Archived | Published | Restore & Publish | Approvers |

A draft cannot go straight to Published. It has to pass through review first.

## Saving

The editor has three ways to save. Save Progress keeps the SOP in its current state, so a SOP in review stays in review. Quick Save does the same and keeps you on the page. Submit for Review saves it and sends it to review.

The editor also autosaves about 5 seconds after the last change. Autosave does not run while the form has a validation error, such as a missing title or no group selected. If someone says their work did not save, that is the first thing to check.

## Editing a published SOP

Clicking Edit on a published SOP does not change the live version. It makes a working copy, and the published SOP stays as it is until that copy is reviewed and published.

Only one working copy can exist at a time. If someone else already started one, the app sends you to theirs with the message "A draft is already in progress." There is no way to take it over and it does not time out, so you need to finish or decline that copy first.

## Versions

Versions are whole numbers. The first publish is 1.0, and every publish after that goes up by one, so 1.0 becomes 2.0. When you publish, you write a short summary of what changed, and it shows in the SOP's revision history along with who published it.

Older versions are kept and can be seen from the Archived page.

## Periodic review

Each SOP has a review interval of 3 months, 6 months or 1 year. When it comes due, the SOP gets an overdue badge. Nothing else changes: it stays published and readable.

The owner gets a reminder 14 days before it is due, on the day it is due, and then once a week while it is overdue. These reminders cannot be turned off. To clear it, open the SOP and use Confirm Periodic Review.

## Copying a SOP

Duplicate makes a new draft called "Copy of" the original, in the same groups. You need to be able to create SOPs in every one of those groups.

Fork copies a SOP into a different group. The copy starts in review, titled "[Fork]" followed by the original title. You need to be able to read the original and create SOPs in the group you are copying to.
