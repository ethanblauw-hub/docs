---
title: "Reviewing and approving"
description: "How a review reaches you, publishing, declining, and named reviewers."
---

When someone submits a SOP for review, the approvers of its groups get a notification. If the author named specific reviewers, only those people are told instead. See [Named reviewers](#named-reviewers) below.

The SOPs In Review card on Home lists everything waiting in your groups.

## Approve & Publish

Open the SOP and click Approve & Publish. The dialog shows the version it will become and asks for a short summary of what changed. That summary goes into the revision history, so write it for someone reading it a year from now.

Publishing makes the new version live, records the review date, and lets the SOP Assistant find the new text. The owner is notified that it was published.

## Decline

Decline sends a SOP back without publishing it. It needs a reason, and the owner is told what it was.

What happens next depends on whether the SOP was ever published. A SOP that has never been published goes back to Draft. A working copy of a published SOP is thrown away, and the live version stays exactly as it was. The author has to start a new copy if they still want to make the change, so put enough in the reason for them to know what to fix.

## Named reviewers

Named reviewers let an author pick who reviews a SOP, instead of leaving it to every approver in the group. It is off unless an admin turns it on, with the "Let authors assign reviewers" switch under Settings, Approval Groups.

When it is on, the author can pick approval groups (reusable lists of people that admins set up), individual people, or both. They also choose whether everyone has to approve or just some of them. They can add an inform list of people who are told when it is published but get no access from it.

The list locks when the SOP is submitted. From then on:

- Only the people on that list can approve it, and approving is separate from publishing.
- Each approval is tied to the exact text that was reviewed. If anyone saves a change after you approve, your approval no longer counts and you need to approve again.
- Publish stays blocked until enough approvals are in, and the message says who it is still waiting on.

An admin can override a blocked publish, but has to type a reason, and the reason is kept in the revision history. An admin who is impersonating someone cannot override.

Turning the switch off stops new SOPs from using named reviewers. Reviews that already started still finish under their own list.

A SOP with no named reviewers works the old way: any approver in its groups can publish it.
