SOP Central is where Egan writes, reviews and publishes its Standard Operating Procedures. A SOP starts as a draft, goes to review, and is published once an approver signs off. Every published version is kept, so you can always see what a SOP said and who approved it.

This guide is for the people who keep it running: approvers, group managers, admins, and anyone who helps maintain the app without working in its code. It explains who can do what, how review works, and where to look when something does not behave the way someone expects.

## Signing in

Sign in with your Egan Google account. A new user with no group lands on a page that asks them to pick a group and request access. Until someone approves that request they can read published SOPs, but they cannot create or edit anything.

## The sidebar

The sidebar has: Home, New View (Preview), Archived, Settings, How-To & Guides, Visual Standards, and Process Maps. Settings only shows for admins. Approvers who manage a group can still reach their part of Settings by going to `/settings` directly.

Home has three cards at the top, My Drafts, SOPs In Review and Published SOPs, and each one links to its full list. Below them is the list of operating groups, with your role in each and a Request Access button for the ones you are not in.

## Where to start

If you are new to approving, read [Roles and permissions](/sop-central/roles) and then [Reviewing and approving](/sop-central/reviewing-and-approving). If you administer users and groups, start with [Users and groups](/sop-central/users-and-groups).

If something in here does not match what you see in the app, let me know. The app changes more often than these pages do.
