Every event shows in the bell tray. Some of them also send an email. The person who caused the event is never notified about it, and an email only goes to people who can actually view the SOP.

## What emails

| Event | Who gets it | Can they turn it off? |
|---|---|---|
| Assigned as a named reviewer | The named reviewers | No |
| SOP submitted for review | The group's approvers, when no reviewers are named | Yes |
| Change request filed | The owner and approvers | Yes |
| Change request published | The person who filed it | Yes |
| Change request declined | The person who filed it | Yes |
| Someone approved my SOP | The owner | Yes, and it starts off |
| My SOP was published | The owner | Yes |
| My SOP was sent back | The owner | No |
| Published, for the inform list | The people on the inform list | Yes |
| Review coming due, due, or overdue | The owner | No |

These only show in the tray and never email: a SOP being published or archived for its readers, branch updates, access requests, work location requests, Visual Standards reviews, and tips.

## The bell tray

The tray has two tabs, Inbox and Archive. Read, archived and dismissed are saved for each person, so they survive a reload and follow you to another device. What you do to a notice never changes it for anyone else who got it.

- Opening a notice, or using its Mark Read button, marks it read. Mark Unread puts it back.
- Archive moves a notice to the Archive tab and marks it read. Restoring it from the Archive tab puts it back in the Inbox as read.
- Mark all read marks every unread notice in the Inbox as read, however many there are.
- Dismiss completely removes the notice from your tray only. Everyone else who got it still has it. Dismiss all does the same for every notice showing on the current tab. Archived notices and notices hidden by the filter stay.
- The bell dot and the Inbox count mean you have unread notices in your Inbox. Hiding a group with the filter does not change them.

The tray updates straight away and then saves. If the save fails, the change is undone and a message says so.

Denying an access request is different from dismissing it. The request has been handled, so it is removed for every approver, not just you.

Review request notices clear on their own once the review is over: when the SOP is published, archived or moved back to draft, when the change request is resolved or declined, or when the SOP is deleted.

While impersonating, changes in the tray only last until you reload. They are not saved to the other person's tray.

## Turning email off

Each person manages their own email at Notification settings, which is linked from the bell tray and from the bottom of every email. The events that cannot be turned off are still listed there, just locked. Tray notifications cannot be turned off.

An admin who is impersonating someone sees their own settings on that page, not the other person's.

## Email Delivery

Admins control whether the app sends email at all, under Settings, Maintenance, Email Delivery. It has three modes:

- Paused. No email is sent.
- Testing. Every email goes to one tester address instead of the real recipients, and the subject names who it was meant for. The tester has to be an Egan address.
- Live. Email goes to the real recipients. It asks you to confirm before switching.

The panel shows who last changed it and when. If it is ever unset, the app treats it as Paused.

Live email only goes to Egan addresses.
