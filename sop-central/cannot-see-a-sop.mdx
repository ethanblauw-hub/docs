---
title: "Someone cannot see a SOP"
description: "The checks to run, in order, when a SOP is missing for someone."
---

This is the most common question. Work through these in order, and most of the time one of the first three is the answer.

1. Have them refresh. Lists load once when a page opens, so a SOP published or shared since then will not be there until they reload. A role change also needs a reload.
2. Check its state. Anyone signed in can read a published SOP. A draft or a SOP in review only shows to its owner, its editors, the Approvers of its groups, and its named reviewers. An archived SOP only shows to members of its groups.
3. Check their role in the SOP's groups. A Viewer or Contributor does not see other people's drafts, only Approvers do. If the SOP belongs only to a subgroup, a Viewer or Contributor on the parent group will not see it.
4. Check work location. A draft or archived SOP marked Field does not show to an Office user, and the other way around. Published SOPs are not affected by that rule.
5. Check the work location filter. SOP lists open narrowed to the viewer's own work location, so a Field user starts on Field and Company-Wide SOPs, and an Office user on Office and Company-Wide. A published Office SOP is still there for a Field user, just hidden from the list. On the Dashboard, a "Showing Field and Company-Wide SOPs" notice shows while the list is narrowed. Have them click Show all locations, or pick All locations under Filter by Work Location. On `/home-v2`, they change the Work location select to All locations.
6. Check the Assistant separately. If they can open the SOP but the SOP Assistant cannot find it, the search may need rebuilding. See [Settings and feature switches](/sop-central/settings-and-switches#maintenance).

The quickest way to confirm what they see is to impersonate them from User Management. See [Impersonation](/sop-central/users-and-groups#impersonation).

If none of these explain it, let me know which SOP and which person, and I will look.
