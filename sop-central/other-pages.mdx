## Archived

Archived lists archived SOPs and older published versions. Approvers can bring an archived SOP back with Restore to Drafts or Restore & Publish. Only members of the SOP's groups can see it once it is archived.

## New View (Preview)

A single searchable table of SOPs with filters for status, group, area and tags. It is still a preview, so it may change.

## Visual Standards

Visual Standards are 5S station standards, with photos and tool lists. They have their own draft, review and publish steps, separate from SOPs. Admins and Approvers can create them.

## Process Maps

Process Maps holds draw.io diagrams, shown as interactive maps. A new map is Company-Wide unless you pick groups, which means everyone can see it. Only admins can move a map to different groups. A deleted map leaves a notice in its place for 30 days.

## How-To & Guides

The in-app help page, with a short explanation of each SOP state and how versions work. It also has the Tips feed: Admins and Approvers can submit a tip, and admins or tips approvers publish them. Nobody can approve their own tip.

## Sharing

The Share dialog adds named editors to a SOP, and lets the owner or an admin hand ownership to someone else.

Every SOP also has a permanent share link. Opening it still needs a sign-in, and the person only sees the SOP if they are allowed to. Sending the link does not give anyone access.

## Printing and exporting

Print uses the template from Settings, Templates and opens the browser's print dialog. Viewers in Industrial Controls cannot print or export its SOPs.
