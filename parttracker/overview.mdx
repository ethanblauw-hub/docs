PartTracker follows prefab shop material from the moment it arrives until it is delivered to a job site. It knows what each job needs, what has been received, where it sits in the shop, what is in production, and what has shipped.

These pages are for managers and for anyone who supports the app. They explain how the app works and why it does what it does. They are not a click-by-click guide for shop floor users.

## The records

A job is set up in the ERP or in Job Create, and both feed the company data warehouse in BigQuery. PartTracker does not create real jobs. It reads them from BigQuery on a schedule and removes them when BigQuery stops listing them. See [Jobs and the BigQuery sync](/parttracker/jobs-and-erp-sync).

A BOM is the list of parts a job needs, imported from a spreadsheet or a PDF. One job can have several BOMs, one per PO. See [BOMs](/parttracker/boms).

A container is anything that physically holds material: a pallet, a box, a cart. Every container belongs to a job (or to the STOCK job) and sits on a shelf location. See [Receiving](/parttracker/receiving) and [Containers and shelves](/parttracker/containers-and-shelves).

A production ticket is a unit of shop work. Most come from the ERP. Material moves from containers into a production ticket, and back out into carts when the work is done. See [Production tickets](/parttracker/production-tickets).

A shipping ticket is one truck run to a site. Carts are attached to it, it gets picked up, and it gets delivered. See [Shipping tickets](/parttracker/shipping-tickets).

## The flow in one line

BOM says what is needed, receiving brings it in, it sits on a shelf, it moves into production, it comes back out on a cart, and the cart ships on a shipping ticket.

Every step changes the stock totals on the Inventory page and leaves a record in the audit log and the material movement history. Nobody edits those records by hand. See [Inventory totals](/parttracker/inventory-totals) and [Audit log and movements](/parttracker/audit-log-and-movements).

## Who can do what

There are six roles, from PM up to Admin, and each one can do everything the roles below it can. See [Roles and permissions](/parttracker/roles-and-permissions).
