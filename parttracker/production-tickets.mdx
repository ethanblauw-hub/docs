---
title: "Production tickets"
description: "Moving material into a production ticket, building carts from it, shipping it, and what happens to voided tickets."
---

A production ticket is a unit of shop work. Material that is being worked on lives on the ticket, not in a container, and it counts as WIP on the Inventory page.

Most tickets come from SIS through BigQuery. See [Jobs and the BigQuery sync](/parttracker/jobs-and-erp-sync) for where they come from and what their statuses mean.

## Material in

Material reaches a ticket three ways: To Production from a container, Add Standalone Item into the Manual Adds ticket, and Smart Pack, which can pull from production items when it builds carts.

## Material out

Move / Assemble on the ticket page (Shop Worker and up) opens Cart Creator / Assembly. It has two choices.

Complete & Ship / Assemble builds carts from the ticket's material. The new carts are Waiting to Ship.

Return to Stock puts the material back into In Stock containers.

You cannot take out more than the ticket holds. That stops with Over-allocation, or with an Insufficient qty message that says how much the ticket has. An empty cart is allowed, but the app asks first.

## Shipping a ticket

Ship Ticket (Shipping/Receiving and up), in the same dialog, finishes the ticket. Every unit has to be in a cart first; otherwise you get Cannot Ship, Items Remaining. The ticket becomes Shipped, and if a shipping ticket had reserved it, the carts join that shipping ticket.

## Editing and deleting

Edit (Shop Lead and up) changes the quantities line by line. Each change is recorded in the movement history as an adjustment. If an edit empties an app-made ticket, the ticket is deleted. An ERP ticket stays, even when it is empty.

Delete Ticket (Shop Lead and up) only works on tickets the app made. ERP tickets cannot be deleted in the app; remove their items instead.

## Voided tickets holding material

When the ERP voids a ticket that still has material on it, nothing moves that material automatically. It still counts as WIP. The Production page shows a Voided Tickets Holding Material card (Shop Lead and up). Return Material on that card moves all of a ticket's material into a stock container on whatever job you choose.

## Travelers

Print Traveler (Shop Lead and up) prints a ticket's traveler on letter paper, or a 4 by 6 pallet label in portrait or landscape. The Tickets list can print several at once. See [Labels, QR codes and travelers](/parttracker/labels-and-travelers).
