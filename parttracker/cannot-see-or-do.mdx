---
title: "Someone cannot see or do something"
description: "The checks to run, in order, when a page, button or record is missing."
---

Work down this list. Most problems stop at the first three.

1. Are they signed in with their company Google account? Any other account is refused when it is first created.
2. What is their role on Access Control? New accounts are PM, the lowest rank. Compare it with [Roles and permissions](/parttracker/roles-and-permissions). If they are not on Access Control at all, run Import Users; see [Users and access](/parttracker/users-and-access).
3. Was their role changed recently? Have them reload, or sign out and back in. They should see Syncing Permissions first.
4. Is the page above their rank? The app quietly sends them back to the Inventory page.
5. Is the button hidden because of the record's status? Mark as Picked Up / Shipped needs carts on the ticket first. Mark Delivered only shows on a Picked up ticket. To Production is not offered on STOCK containers or Waiting to Ship ones. Delete Ticket does not show on synced tickets. Return Material only applies to voided tickets. Editing a shipping ticket after pickup is Admin only.
6. Does the button show, but fail when pressed? A few buttons show for a role that cannot finish the job. See the list below.
7. Is the record really missing? Closed jobs are hidden on Jobs until you turn on the closed filter, and a Closed job cannot be imported. A new ERP ticket can take up to 12 hours to arrive; Import Tickets brings it in now. My Jobs is empty for anyone whose account is not linked to an employee ID.
8. Is a total wrong? Press Rebuild Inventory first. See [Inventory totals](/parttracker/inventory-totals).
9. Is an email missing? See the checklist at the bottom of [Notifications](/parttracker/notifications).

## Buttons that show for a role that cannot use them

These should be cleaned up in the app. Until then, the person sees the button, presses it, and gets a permission error.

| Button | Shows for | Actually needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Add Standalone Item | Shop Lead | Shipping/Receiving |
| Delete on a Waiting for Delivery shipping ticket | Everyone | Shop Manager |
| Mark as Picked Up / Shipped | Everyone who can open the ticket | Shipping/Receiving |
| Create Ticket on Shipping | Shop Worker | Shipping/Receiving |
| Saving the Delivery Type Routing card | Shipping/Receiving | Admin |
