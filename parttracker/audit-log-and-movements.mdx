---
title: "Audit log and movements"
description: "The two histories PartTracker keeps, who can see them, and how a correction works."
---

PartTracker keeps two histories, and nobody can edit either one from the app. Only the app's server writes them.

## Audit log

The audit log records changes to records: what changed on a container, a ticket or a BOM, who did it, and when. The Audit Log Dashboard (Shipping/Receiving and up, under Admin) has tabs for Tickets, BOMs, Containers and a Timeline, with a legend explaining the entry types. View Change Log on a container or ticket shows the same entries for just that record.

The person recorded on an entry is normally the person who pressed the button. Changes the app makes on its own, like the ERP sync or a totals rebuild, can show the last person who edited that record instead, so do not read a name on an automatic change as that person having done it.

## Material movements

Movements tracks material instead of records: one row for each part each time it moves, with where it came from and where it went. Everyone can see it. The page starts on your own jobs, with a switch to show all of them. Each part also has a trace page that follows it from receipt onward.

## Correcting a movement

Shop Leads and up can correct a movement when the wrong thing was recorded. A correction does not rewind history. It records a new move, from wherever the material is now to where it should be, with a reason. The original row stays, so the history shows both the mistake and the fix.
