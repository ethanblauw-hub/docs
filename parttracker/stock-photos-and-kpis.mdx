## Stock

Material that is not tied to a real job lives on a stock job, most often STOCK. Add Standalone Item (Add to Stock) creates a stock container. Shipping/Receiving and up can do that.

The Stock Hub (Shop Lead and up) transfers stock material to a real job. Each transfer is also written to the accounting stock sheet, so accounting sees it without anyone retyping it. Transfers from the old STOCK job are not written to the sheet.

## Photos

Add Photo on a container, a production ticket or a shipping ticket takes a picture with the camera, uploads a file, or picks one from Google Photos.

On a shipping ticket, the truck photos are taken at pickup and the delivery photo at delivery. When Shipment Photos Links is turned on in Settings, a ticket's photos can be shared by link with people outside the app. See [Settings and switches](/parttracker/settings).

## Shipping KPIs

Shipping KPIs (Shop Manager and up) only counts delivered tickets, and it uses Central time for days. The tiles are: tickets delivered, pickup notice lead time, pickup variance, on-time pickup rate, on-time delivery rate, and deck footprint. You can filter by department, job, shipping type and date range.
