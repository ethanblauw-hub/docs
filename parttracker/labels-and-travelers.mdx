## Container labels

Print Label on a container prints its label with a QR code. Receiving offers the labels for every new container as soon as the receipt is saved.

## Shelf labels

Shelf QR Codes prints labels for the shelf locations.

## Scanning

Scanning a container or shelf QR code with a hardware scanner, anywhere in the app, jumps to that container or shelf. The app confirms with Container scanned or Shelf scanned.

## Travelers

Print Traveler on a production ticket (Shop Lead and up) has three sizes: Traveler (8.5" × 11"), Pallet label in portrait (4" × 6"), and Pallet label in landscape (6" × 4"). The Tickets list can print several tickets at once. The job details on a traveler come from the job sync, so a wrong job name on a traveler is usually wrong upstream too, in the ERP or Job Create.

## Shipping paperwork

On a shipping ticket, Print Manifest prints the manifest with its delivery QR code, or Print Labels when there is more than one cart. View QRs shows them on screen. See [Shipping tickets](/parttracker/shipping-tickets).
